VodafoneThree, Nokia partner to trial AI-powered mobile networks

The operator will test Nokia’s AI-native anyRAN software, running on Nvidia’s Aerial RAN computer, first in the lab and then on selected UK mobile sites.

By Jasdip SensiEditor · 18 November 2026 · 3 min read

VodafoneThree has partnered with Nokia to test how AI could help make its mobile network faster and more reliable.

As a result, the telecom giants plan to carry out a series of trials in the UK, starting in a lab before moving to selected mobile sites.

The tests will use Nokia’s AI-native anyRAN software, running on Nvidia Aerial RAN computer, to see how AI can improve network performance and make better use of available spectrum.

The trials will look at how AI can help the network respond to changing conditions. For example, it could identify when a connection is weakening and automatically adjust how data is sent to keep customers connected and improve their experience.

“These technologies could help the network more accurately assess changing radio conditions and adapt how data is transmitted in real time, helping to improve network performance and the user experience,” Vodafone stated.

VodafoneThree and Nokia will also look at how the same computing technology could be used to run both the mobile network and AI services.

“AI-RAN represents an exciting next step in the evolution of our network, using AI to make connectivity more intelligent, responsive and efficient.”Iain Milligan, Network Development and Infrastructure Director, VodafoneThree

VodafoneThree, Network Development and Infrastructure Director, Iain Milligan, said: “AI-RAN represents an exciting next step in the evolution of our network, using AI to make connectivity more intelligent, responsive and efficient. Working with Nokia, we’ll test these capabilities to understand how AI can improve network performance and, ultimately, the experience we deliver for our customers.

“As our 5G Standalone network evolves, we see significant potential for AI-RAN to help us respond more dynamically to different customer needs and unlock new, differentiated connectivity experiences, while laying the foundations for the next generation of intelligent, AI-native networks.”

Nokia President of Mobile Infrastructure, Emma Falck, added: “This collaboration with VodafoneThree is an important step in moving AI-RAN from industry ambition toward commercial reality.

“By combining Nokia’s AI-RAN innovations with Nvidia accelerated computing, we can help VodafoneThree enhance radio performance and network efficiency today while building a software-driven foundation for new edge-AI services and AI-native 6G. It is a practical approach that protects existing network investments and creates room for continuous innovation.”
