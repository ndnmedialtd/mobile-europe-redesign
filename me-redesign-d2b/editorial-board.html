About Mobile EuropeEditorial Advisory Board

Mobile Europe is privileged and proud to work with our diverse board of advisors to inform its editorial understanding and coverage. Their time, wisdom and input help us evolve the brand to meet our audience’s constantly changing needs, both our daily, written output and virtual events.

We focus on the ecosystem around all kinds of communications infrastructure, including strategies and technological evolution. We do not view technology as an end in itself, but in terms of the business and operational models it enables.

Our coverage mainly concerns Europe, the Middle East and Africa, but we recognise that telecoms is, in every sense, an interconnected global industry.
