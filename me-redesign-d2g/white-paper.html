There’s a Core for That

Turn your mobile network core into a growth engine for new revenue, richer customer experiences and superior network performance.

Published by
Mavenir
Format
PDF · 18 pages
Published
September 2026
Topics
Cloud/NFV, 5G & Beyond

Operators have spent heavily on 5G, but for many the core network is still treated as plumbing: a cost to be kept running rather than a platform to build on. This paper argues the opposite, and shows how a cloud-native, automated core can open new revenue and lift the customer experience.

What you’ll learn

  • How a cloud-native core cuts the time to launch new services from months to days
  • Where network slicing and APIs create new enterprise revenue
  • How automation and AI reduce operating costs and improve reliability
  • A practical migration path from legacy core to 5G Standalone

Who should read it

CTOs, network strategy and architecture teams, and product leaders at mobile operators and MVNOs across EMEA.
