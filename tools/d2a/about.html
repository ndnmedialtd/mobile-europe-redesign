About Mobile EuropeEditorial Advisory Board

Mobile Europe is the leading B2B title for the telecoms industry, exploring operators’ technology strategies and providing CTOs and their teams in Europe and MEA with news, analysis and opinion about the latest developments in the sector.

Its coverage spans all aspects of connectivity, the use of AI and automation by network operators, 5G/6G, the Internet of Things, fibre broadband, digital transformation, network virtualisation and infrastructure, and many other key technologies affecting the transforming telco space.

Across its website, virtual Briefings, Independent Research reports, White Papers, email newsletters, webinars and social media presence, Mobile Europe provides a comprehensive guide to what’s happening in the industry and where its future lies.

We are 100 percent independent and editorially led so we can guarantee impartial analysis of the key announcements and trends in the wireless space.

Mobile Europe has been established for over 20 years. We are able to offer thought-provoking content through numerous innovative channels, providing readers and advertisers with a unique mix to meet their marketing needs.
