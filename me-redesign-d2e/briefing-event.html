AI in Network Optimisation: Finding Forgotten Revenue

After a long period of heavy investment in infrastructure, including 5G capability and data centres, operators are determined to boost ROI and profitability, and they are looking to AI as a way of achieving this.

There are already numerous examples of AI being used to help operators increase efficiencies and save cash: from fraud prevention and reducing customer service costs, to energy optimisation and better network management.

Use cases are also emerging of AI playing a role in boosting existing revenue streams and creating new income, such as offering enterprise customers access to AI infrastructure, building 5G SA networks customised to their needs, and serving customers in ways not possible before.

This Briefing looks at operators’ attitudes and progress toward these opportunities, what other options are appearing on the horizon, the drivers, what’s holding them back and what needs to happen next.
